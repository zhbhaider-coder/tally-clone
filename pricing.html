Questions & answers

Is Tally really free?

Yes — unlimited forms and submissions are free within fair-use limits. You only pay if you want branding removal, custom domains, collaboration or analytics.

Are Tally forms secure?

Yes. Form data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and you control the information you collect.

How does Tally compare to other form builders?

Most builders limit submissions or lock advanced features behind premium plans. Tally gives you unlimited forms and submissions for free, with conditional logic, signatures and password protection included.
