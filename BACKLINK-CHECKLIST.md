# Backlink checklist — tally-clone.pages.dev

**Status: NOT submitted.** These are the site owner's taps — each listing needs his account and a real, human-written description. Do them one at a time, in order. The full plan and rules live in `backlinks.md` (still present — 10 ready-to-submit listings, plus a second batch).

**Rules (standing):** legitimate levers only. No paid links, no link farms, no spam, no scraped outreach, no automated submissions. One good link from a real directory beats fifty spammy ones.

**What to paste everywhere:**
- **URL:** `https://tally-clone.pages.dev/`
- **Name:** Tally — Free form builder
- **One-line description (adapt, don't spin):** "Free online form builder with a Notion-style editor — unlimited forms and submissions, conditional logic, and a shareable link. No signup needed to try it."
