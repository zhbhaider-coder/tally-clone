Questions & answers

Is Tally really free?

Yes. Unlimited forms and unlimited submissions are free, within fair-use limits. Paid plans add branding removal, custom domains, collaboration and analytics.

Are Tally forms secure?

Yes. Form data is encrypted both in transit and at rest, and you stay in control of the information you collect.

How does Tally compare to other form builders?

Unlike most form builders that limit submissions or lock advanced features behind premium plans, Tally offers unlimited forms and submissions for free — with conditional logic, signatures and password protection included.
